What Are Dental Implants?

Dental implants represent the gold standard in tooth replacement. These titanium posts are surgically placed into your jawbone, where they fuse with the bone tissue through a process called osseointegration. Once integrated, the implant serves as an artificial tooth root, providing a stable foundation for a crown, bridge, or denture. Unlike traditional dentures that rest on the gums or bridges that require alteration of adjacent teeth, implants function independently and preserve the integrity of your natural teeth.

The structure of a dental implant consists of three components: the titanium post that anchors into the jawbone, an abutment that connects to the post, and the visible restoration (crown, bridge, or denture) that completes your smile. This three-part system delivers unmatched stability and longevity. According to research published in the Journal of Family Medicine and Primary Care, dental implants have a survival rate of 95% over five years, making them one of the most reliable solutions in modern dentistry.

Benefits of Choosing Dental Implants

The advantages of dental implants extend far beyond aesthetics. First, implants preserve your jawbone. When you lose a tooth, the bone in that area begins to deteriorate due to lack of stimulation. Implants provide the necessary stimulation to maintain bone density, protecting your facial structure and preventing the sunken appearance often associated with tooth loss. Second, implants restore full chewing function. You can enjoy your favorite foods without worry, from crunchy apples to chewy steaks. The stability of implants eliminates the slipping and discomfort common with traditional dentures.

Third, implants protect your remaining teeth. Unlike traditional bridges, which require grinding down healthy adjacent teeth for support, implants stand alone. This conservative approach preserves your natural tooth structure. Fourth, implants offer exceptional durability. With proper care and maintenance, they can last decades or even a lifetime. This longevity makes them a cost-effective investment in your oral health. Finally, implants feel and function like natural teeth. You won’t need to worry about removing them for cleaning or adjusting to artificial sensations. They become a seamless part of your smile.

The Dental Implant Process

Initial Consultation and Treatment Planning

Your implant journey begins with a comprehensive evaluation at our St. George office. We use advanced imaging technology to assess your jawbone density, determine the optimal implant placement, and create a customized treatment plan. During this appointment, we’ll discuss your medical history, review your restoration goals, and answer all your questions about the procedure.

Surgical Placement

The implant placement procedure is typically performed under local anesthesia to ensure your comfort. Dr. Davis carefully positions the titanium post into the prepared site in your jawbone. For patients experiencing dental anxiety, we offer sedation options to help you relax throughout the procedure. The surgery itself usually takes one to two hours per implant, depending on the complexity of your case.

Healing and Osseointegration

After placement, the implant needs time to integrate with your jawbone. This healing period typically lasts three to six months, during which the bone grows around the implant to secure it firmly in place. We may provide a temporary restoration to protect the area and maintain your appearance during this phase.

Final Restoration

Once osseointegration is complete, we attach the abutment and place your custom-made crown, bridge, or denture. The restoration is carefully matched to the color, shape, and size of your natural teeth, creating a seamless blend with your smile. We’ll make any necessary adjustments to ensure proper bite alignment and comfort.

Candidates for Dental Implants

Most adults with missing teeth are candidates for dental implants. However, successful implant placement requires adequate jawbone density to support the posts. If you’ve experienced bone loss, we may recommend bone grafting procedures or alternative treatment approaches. Ideal candidates maintain good oral hygiene, have healthy gums, and are committed to maintaining their implants through regular dental visits and home care.

Certain medical conditions may affect implant candidacy. Uncontrolled diabetes, active gum disease, heavy smoking, or ongoing cancer treatment can impact healing and integration. During your consultation, we’ll review your health history to determine if implants are right for you. For patients who aren’t immediate candidates, we’ll explore solutions to address any obstacles, such as periodontal treatment or lifestyle modifications.

Alternative Tooth Replacement Options

While dental implants offer superior benefits, we understand they may not be suitable for everyone. Traditional dentures provide a non-surgical option for replacing multiple missing teeth. Modern dentures have improved significantly in appearance and comfort, offering a viable solution for patients seeking immediate results or those who prefer to avoid surgery. Fixed bridges represent another alternative, using adjacent teeth as anchors to support a prosthetic tooth. This option works well for replacing single teeth when the neighboring teeth are healthy and strong.

For patients seeking the stability of implants with faster treatment times, we also offer All-on-4 implant-supported dentures. This innovative approach uses just four strategically placed implants to support a full arch of replacement teeth, often allowing for same-day tooth replacement. During your consultation, we’ll discuss all available options and help you choose the solution that best fits your needs, budget, and timeline.
