How Invisalign Works

Invisalign uses a series of custom-made, clear plastic aligners to gradually shift your teeth into their desired positions. Each set of aligners is worn for about one to two weeks before moving to the next set in the series. The aligners are designed using advanced 3D imaging technology that maps out your entire treatment plan from start to finish, allowing you to see a virtual representation of how your smile will transform over time.

Unlike traditional braces that require frequent tightening appointments, Invisalign check-ins typically occur every six to eight weeks. During these visits, Dr. Davis monitors your progress and provides you with your next set of aligners. The treatment timeline varies depending on your specific needs, but many patients achieve their desired results in 12 to 18 months.

Benefits of Choosing Invisalign

Virtually Invisible Appearance

The clear aligners are barely noticeable when worn, making them an excellent choice for professionals, students, and anyone who wants to straighten their teeth without drawing attention to their orthodontic treatment. Research from the American Association of Orthodontists shows that approximately 1 in 3 orthodontic patients are adults, many of whom choose Invisalign specifically for its discreet appearance.

Removable Convenience

You can remove your aligners for eating, drinking, brushing, and flossing, which means no food restrictions and easier oral hygiene maintenance. This flexibility allows you to enjoy Southern Utah’s dining scene without worrying about damaging brackets or getting food stuck in wires. Simply take out your aligners before meals and pop them back in afterward.

Comfortable Treatment Experience

Invisalign aligners are made from smooth plastic with no sharp edges or protruding metal parts. There’s no need for uncomfortable wire adjustments, and the gradual tooth movement typically causes less discomfort than traditional braces. Many patients report only mild pressure during the first few days of wearing a new set of aligners.

What to Expect During Your Invisalign Journey

Your Invisalign treatment at Virgin River Dental begins with a comprehensive consultation where Dr. Davis evaluates your teeth, discusses your smile goals, and determines if you’re a good candidate for clear aligner therapy. Digital scans or impressions of your teeth are taken to create a precise 3D model, which is used to design your custom treatment plan.

Once your aligners are ready, you’ll receive several sets at once along with detailed instructions on wear time and care. Most patients wear their aligners for 20 to 22 hours per day, removing them only for eating and oral hygiene. As you progress through your treatment, you’ll notice gradual changes in your smile alignment, with periodic check-ins ensuring everything stays on track.

Invisalign can address a wide range of orthodontic issues, including crowding, spacing, overbites, underbites, and crossbites. Caring for Your Invisalign Aligners

Maintaining your aligners is straightforward and requires minimal effort. Rinse them with lukewarm water each time you remove them, and clean them daily using a soft toothbrush and clear, antibacterial soap or the official Invisalign cleaning system. Avoid using hot water, which can warp the plastic, and never use toothpaste to clean your aligners, as it can create scratches that make them more visible.

Store your aligners in their protective case whenever they’re not in your mouth to prevent loss or damage. Keep up with your regular brushing and flossing routine to maintain optimal oral health throughout treatment. Dr. Davis and the team at Virgin River Dental provide detailed care instructions and are always available to answer questions about maintaining your aligners.
