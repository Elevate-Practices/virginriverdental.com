What Your UnitedHealthcare Plan Covers

Most UnitedHealthcare dental plans follow a three-tier structure, with preventive care receiving the best coverage. Preventive services often get 100% coverage, meaning you pay nothing out of pocket for routine checkups, professional cleanings, and X-rays.

Basic restorative work like fillings and root canals typically receives 70-80% coverage after you meet your annual deductible. Major procedures, including crowns, bridges, and implants, are normally 40-50% covered. Your specific percentages depend on whether your employer.

PPO vs. HMO: Choosing Your Plan Type

UnitedHealthcare offers both PPO and HMO dental plans across Utah, and picking the right one affects how you access care. PPO plans give you the flexibility to visit any licensed dentist, though you save money staying in-network through negotiated rates. You can even see out-of-network dentists and still receive partial reimbursement, just at lower percentages.

HMO plans require choosing a primary dental office and getting referrals for specialist care. Monthly premiums cost less, but your provider choices stay limited to the HMO network. These plans work well for families seeking predictable copayments and consistent dental homes for their children.

Making Your Annual Maximum Work Harder

UnitedHealthcare sets annual spending limits, usually between $1,000 and $2,000 per person, depending on your plan. Once you hit that maximum, you’re responsible for 100% of additional dental costs until your benefits reset. This makes timing important for expensive treatments.

A strategy many patients miss is splitting extensive dental work across two calendar years. Getting a crown in December and a bridge in January means using two separate annual maximums instead of exhausting one. Our financial coordinators help you plan treatment sequences that make clinical sense while stretching your insurance dollars further.

Special UnitedHealthcare Programs You Might Not Know About

UnitedHealthcare offers wellness incentives for members managing chronic conditions like diabetes or heart disease. These programs may include extra preventive visits or enhanced periodontal coverage, recognizing how oral health affects overall wellness. Pregnant members sometimes qualify for additional cleanings since hormonal changes increase gum disease risk.

Some employer plans include orthodontic benefits covering Invisalign for adults and children. Coverage typically ranges from $1,000-$2,000 lifetime maximums, significantly reducing cosmetic dentistry costs while improving both function and appearance.

Virtual dental consultations through UnitedHealthcare’s telehealth options provide convenient preliminary assessments for minor concerns. These video visits help determine whether you need an in-office appointment, saving time and using your insurance benefits efficiently for appropriate care levels.
