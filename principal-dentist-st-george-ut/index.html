How Principal Structures Dental Coverage

Principal organizes benefits into three distinct categories based on treatment complexity. Each category carries different reimbursement rates designed to encourage regular maintenance while providing financial protection for more expensive procedures. Your employer or individual plan determines specific percentages, but most Principal policies follow similar patterns.

Annual benefit periods run from January through December for most members. This calendar-year structure means unused coverage doesn’t roll forward, making early-year preventive visits financially smart. Deductibles reset annually as well, though certain fourth-quarter provisions may reduce this burden for ongoing treatment.

What Your Preventive Benefits Cover

Principal emphasizes routine maintenance by covering preventive services at higher rates than other dental services. Most plans cover 100% of these appointments, eliminating out-of-pocket costs when you visit in-network providers. Regular preventive care catches small problems before they require expensive intervention.

Your preventive benefits typically include the following:

  • Professional cleanings: Plaque and calculus removal performed by licensed hygienists
  • Periodic examinations: Comprehensive evaluation of teeth, gums, bite, and soft tissues
  • Necessary X-rays: X-ray imaging to identify decay, infection, and bone loss
  • Protective sealants: Resin coatings that shield molar chewing surfaces from bacteria
  • Topical fluoride: Mineral treatments that strengthen enamel against acid attacks

Most adults should schedule dental cleanings every six months to maintain optimal oral health. Principal coverage supports this recommendation with full coverage for biannual visits.

Basic and Major Service Reimbursement

Treatments beyond preventive care fall into basic or major categories. Basic procedures address common problems such as cavities, infections requiring root canals, and damaged teeth requiring extraction. Principal typically covers 70-80% of basic treatment costs after you meet your annual deductible.

Major services include dental crowns that protect weakened teeth, bridges that replace missing teeth, and dentures that restore full function. These procedures usually receive 50% coverage following deductible payments. The higher patient responsibility reflects the increased complexity and laboratory costs associated with major dental work.

Planning Treatment Around Benefit Cycles

Smart scheduling reduces total out-of-pocket expenses for extensive dental needs. Beginning treatment late in the year and completing it in January spreads costs across two benefit maximums rather than exhausting a single year’s coverage. Our financial coordinators review your remaining benefits and recommend timing that balances clinical priorities with insurance optimization.

Pre-determination requests provide written confirmation of coverage before we begin treatment. We send detailed procedure plans to Principal, and they respond with exact reimbursement amounts. This eliminates billing surprises and helps you budget appropriately for your share of costs.

The Fourth-Quarter Deductible Advantage

Principal offers a deductible credit feature that benefits members receiving care between October and December. Deductible payments made during these months count toward both the current year and the next year’s deductible requirement. This policy prevents you from paying two separate deductibles for continuous treatment that spans the calendar year boundary.

Understanding this credit helps you time multi-appointment procedures strategically. If treatment begins in November and continues through February, your October-December deductible payments reduce what you owe for January and February appointments. Our team tracks these credits automatically to ensure you receive this benefit when applicable.

Why Network Participation Matters

Choosing an in-network dentist like Virgin River Dental locks in Principal’s negotiated fee schedules. These predetermined rates protect you from balance billing, where providers charge more than your insurance allows and you pay the difference. In-network agreements contractually limit what we can charge Principal members for covered services.

Direct billing arrangements further simplify your experience. We file electronic claims immediately, track processing, and handle any questions Principal raises about submitted procedures. You pay only your estimated copayment at the appointment rather than paying full price and waiting for reimbursement checks.
