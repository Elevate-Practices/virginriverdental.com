How the membership works

Did you know that we offer an in-house dental savings plan for both new and existing patients? We understand that traditional dental insurance premiums are expensive, and that insurance benefits are extremely limited on most plans. The good news is that as a Virgin River Dental Member all of your preventive care (including cleanings, x-rays and exams) is 100% covered in our office – and you’ll also receive valuable member benefits on restorative care, like fillings, crowns and implants… even cosmetic dentistry!

We know that paying out of pocket can be expensive, so we’ve created a Virgin River Dental Membership Plan so you can get the dental benefits you want and need. Now you can afford dentistry – and have the smile you’ve always wanted. Ask us today about the membership and how it can make your smile affordable. Call our office today and start saving on quality dental care today.

The Virgin River Dental Membership is an annual reduced-fee savings plan for individuals and families that allows all members to receive quality dental services at greatly reduced prices. Unlike conventional insurance plans, there are no deductibles, no yearly maximums, and no waiting periods to begin treatment.
