What Are All-on-4 Dental Implants?

All-on-4 is a revolutionary dental implant technique that anchors a full arch of replacement teeth on just four titanium implants. By angling the posterior implants to maximize contact with available bone, this approach often eliminates the need for bone grafting procedures that can add months to treatment time. The result is a fixed, non-removable set of teeth that looks and functions like natural teeth.

This innovative system differs from traditional dentures in every important way. While dentures rest on your gums and require adhesives, All-on-4 teeth are permanently secured to implants fused with your jawbone. This means no slipping, no dietary restrictions, and no nightly removal. The implants also preserve your bone structure, preventing the facial collapse that occurs with tooth loss and denture wear.

How All-on-4 Differs from Traditional Solutions

Superior to Dentures

Traditional dentures float on your gums, causing sore spots, limiting food choices, and often embarrassing slippage. All-on-4 provides rock-solid stability that eliminates these problems entirely. You’ll bite with up to 70% of natural tooth function compared to just 20% with dentures. No more adhesives, no more covering your mouth when you laugh, no more avoiding your favorite foods.

More Efficient Than Individual Implants

Replacing each missing tooth with an individual implant would require 14-16 implants per arch, extensive surgery, and significant cost. All-on-4 achieves full arch restoration with just four strategically placed implants, reducing surgery time, healing periods, and overall investment while delivering equally impressive results.

Immediate Function

Unlike traditional implant approaches requiring months of healing before tooth attachment, All-on-4 provides temporary teeth the same day as surgery. You leave our office with a beautiful, functional smile, never experiencing a day without teeth. These immediate teeth allow normal function while your implants integrate with your bone.

The All-on-4 Treatment Process

Your transformation begins with detailed 3D imaging using our cone beam technology. This reveals your bone structure, allowing us to plan optimal implant placement for maximum stability and aesthetics. We create a surgical guide ensuring precise implant positioning while designing your new smile to complement your facial features perfectly.

On surgery day, we gently remove any remaining teeth and place four implants per arch according to our digital plan. The angled posterior implants utilize available bone in areas typically requiring grafting with traditional techniques. Your pre-made temporary teeth attach immediately to the implants, allowing you to leave with a functional smile the same day. Over the next few months, your implants fuse with your bone while you enjoy your temporary teeth.

Once healing is complete, we create your final restoration using the highest quality materials. These permanent teeth are stronger and more aesthetic than the temporaries, custom-designed to provide optimal function and natural appearance. The entire process typically completes within 4-6 months, transforming your oral health and quality of life.
