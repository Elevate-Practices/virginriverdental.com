Your dentists

Meet the doctors

Dr. Brett Davis, DMD

Dentist and Owner

Dr. Brett Davis completed his Bachelor’s Degree in Biology from Brigham Young University in Idaho and earned his DMD degree from the University of Nevada, Las Vegas School of Dental Medicine. Dr. Davis is a member of the American Dental Association and volunteer experiences include Huntridge Teen Clinic, Give Kids A Smile, Sergeant Clint Ferrin Dental Clinic, Boy Scout leader, and Boy Scout committee chairman. He also provided international humanitarian dental work in Ecuador.

Dr. Davis and his wife love spending time with their 3 kids. Most of their free time is centered on physical and outdoor activities, including everything from camping, swimming, and bungee jumping in the summer to skiing in the winter.

Dr. John DeMille, DDS

Dentist

Originally from Cedar City, Utah, Dr. DeMille graduated Summa Cum Laude from Southern Utah University before earning his dental degree from The Ohio State University. He then completed a General Practice Residency at the University of Utah Hospital, where he received advanced training in endodontics, sedation, oral surgery, prosthodontics, and implantology.

Outside of dentistry, Dr. DeMille enjoys spending time with his wife and three children, exploring the outdoors, riding single-track trails on his dirt bike, and fishing. He also has a passion for hands-on and creative pursuits, including auto mechanics, construction, and 3D printing.
