What Is Professional Teeth Whitening?

Professional teeth whitening uses prescription-strength bleaching agents to penetrate tooth enamel and break down deep stains at the molecular level. Unlike store-bought strips or trays that sit loosely on teeth with weak formulas, professional treatment delivers concentrated whitening power exactly where needed. The active ingredients, typically hydrogen peroxide or carbamide peroxide, are carefully formulated to maximize results while protecting your tooth structure.

The key difference between professional and DIY whitening lies in customization and safety. Our whitening systems are tailored to your specific needs, taking into account your tooth sensitivity, existing restorations, and desired shade goals. We monitor the entire process to ensure even results without damaging your enamel or irritating your gums. This professional oversight prevents the common problems of over-whitening, uneven results, or severe sensitivity that often occur with unsupervised treatments.

How Much Is Teeth Whitening in St. George, Utah?

In St. George, teeth whitening costs between $85 and $499. The price varies based on whether you choose in-office professional whitening or take-home whitening kits.

Professional Whitening Options

Our practice offers two professional whitening approaches, each with unique advantages. In-office power whitening provides immediate dramatic results perfect for upcoming events or when you want instant gratification. Using advanced light activation or higher concentration gels, we can brighten your smile by up to eight shades in about an hour. Throughout treatment, we protect your gums and monitor your comfort, adjusting as needed to prevent sensitivity.

Take-home professional whitening offers convenience and gradual results using custom-fitted trays and professional-grade gel. After taking precise impressions, we create trays that fit your teeth perfectly, ensuring even whitening without gel leaking onto gums. You’ll wear these trays for specified periods over 10-14 days, achieving professional results at your own pace. Many patients appreciate this option’s flexibility and the ability to touch up their smile whenever needed.
