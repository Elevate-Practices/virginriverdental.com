What Are Dental Crowns?

Dental crowns are custom-made caps that cover the entire visible portion of a damaged tooth, restoring its shape, size, strength, and appearance. Think of a crown as protective armor for your tooth – it shields the vulnerable structure underneath while allowing you to bite and chew normally. Modern crowns are crafted from durable materials that mimic natural tooth enamel, providing both protection and aesthetics.

Unlike fillings that repair a portion of your tooth, crowns encase the entire tooth above the gum line. This comprehensive coverage makes them ideal for teeth that have lost significant structure due to decay, fracture, or previous dental work. By redistributing biting forces evenly across the tooth, crowns prevent further damage while restoring full function. Today’s crown materials and techniques create results so natural that even you might forget which tooth has been crowned.

When Are Dental Crowns Necessary?

Large Cavities and Decay

When decay destroys more than half of a tooth’s structure, a filling alone cannot provide adequate strength. Large fillings actually weaken the remaining tooth structure and often lead to fractures. A crown reinforces the entire tooth, preventing breaks while eliminating decay. This protective approach saves teeth that might otherwise require extraction.

Cracked or Fractured Teeth

Cracks in teeth tend to worsen over time, potentially splitting the tooth completely. Whether from trauma, grinding, or biting hard objects, these fractures cause pain and sensitivity that disrupts daily life. Crowns hold the tooth together, preventing crack progression while eliminating discomfort. Early crown placement often makes the difference between saving and losing a tooth.

After Root Canal Treatment

Root canal therapy removes infected tissue from inside the tooth, leaving it hollow and brittle. Without living tissue to maintain moisture, these teeth become prone to fracture. A crown is essential after root canal treatment, providing structural support that allows the tooth to function normally for decades.

The Crown Process at Virgin River Dental

Your crown journey begins with a thorough examination using digital x-rays and sometimes 3D imaging to assess your tooth’s condition. We remove any decay and shape the tooth to create ideal space for your crown. Using our advanced digital impression system, we capture precise tooth measurements without messy traditional molds. This technology ensures your crown fits perfectly from day one.

For many crowns, our same-day crown technology eliminates the wait and temporary crown phase. Using CAD/CAM technology, we design and mill your permanent crown right in our office while you relax. The computer-designed crown is carved from a solid block of ceramic, creating incredible strength and aesthetics. After customizing the color and making final adjustments, we permanently bond your new crown – all in a single visit.
