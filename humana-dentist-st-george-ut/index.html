What Your Plan Typically Covers

Humana structures coverage in tiers based on the type of care you need. Preventive services form the foundation of most plans because they keep your teeth and gums healthy year-round.

Most Humana plans cover 100% of the following preventive services:

  • Routine exams: Comprehensive evaluations checking for cavities, gum disease, and other concerns twice per year
  • Professional cleanings: Removing plaque and tartar buildup that brushing alone can’t address
  • X-rays: Digital imaging that reveals problems beneath the surface, from decay between teeth to bone loss
  • Fluoride applications: Strengthening tooth enamel to resist decay, particularly beneficial for children and adults at higher cavity risk
  • Protective sealants: Thin coatings applied to back teeth to prevent decay in vulnerable grooves and pits


Basic procedures like fillings, routine extractions, and root canal therapy usually receive 70-80% coverage after you meet your deductible. Major services, including crowns, bridges, and dentures, typically have 40-50% coverage depending on your specific plan.

Getting the Most from Your Humana Benefits

Smart planning helps you stretch your insurance dollars further. Most Humana plans follow the calendar year, meaning your benefits reset every January. Scheduling preventive visits early in the year ensures you use these covered services while leaving your annual maximum available for unexpected needs.

Your annual maximum is the total amount Humana pays for dental care each year. If you need extensive treatment, spreading procedures across two benefit years can reduce your financial responsibility. Our financial coordinators create treatment schedules that align with your coverage timeline while addressing your oral health priorities.

For larger procedures, we submit pre-treatment estimates to Humana before starting work. This process gives you a clear breakdown of what your insurance will cover and what you’ll pay out of pocket. Having this information upfront removes uncertainty and helps you budget accordingly.

Why In-Network Care Saves You Money

Choosing an in-network provider like Virgin River Dental offers significant advantages. We’ve agreed to accept Humana’s negotiated rates as full payment for covered services. This means you won’t receive balance bills beyond your expected coinsurance or copays.

We handle all claim submissions electronically, which speeds up processing and reimbursement. Before each appointment, we verify your current coverage and remaining benefits. This real-time confirmation prevents surprises and ensures you know your financial responsibility before receiving treatment.

Special Humana Programs and Member Benefits

Some Humana plans reward consistent preventive care with loyalty benefits. Members who maintain regular dental visits may qualify for increased annual maximums or lower coinsurance rates on major services over time. Ask us about your plan’s loyalty features during your next visit.

Humana’s Dental Savings Plus program provides discounts on services beyond standard coverage. If you’re interested in cosmetic treatments, like teeth whitening or veneers, these supplemental savings help make elective procedures more affordable.

The MyHumana member portal and mobile app let you access your benefits anytime, anywhere. You can review your coverage details, track claims, estimate treatment costs, and find participating providers. These digital tools put your insurance information at your fingertips for convenient benefit management.

Enhanced Coverage for Chronic Health Conditions

Humana recognizes how oral health affects overall wellness. Members with certain chronic conditions, like diabetes or heart disease, may qualify for additional preventive visits each year. These enhanced benefits support your total health by addressing the connection between oral bacteria and systemic conditions.

Expectant mothers can access extra cleanings during and after pregnancy through special maternity benefits. Research shows that pregnancy increases the risk of gum inflammation and other oral health issues, making these additional preventive visits valuable for both maternal and infant health.
