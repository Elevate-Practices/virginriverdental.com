Questions

Good to know before your visit

Do you take my insurance?

We are in network with all major PPO plans, including Delta Dental, Cigna, Aetna, MetLife, Guardian, Blue Cross Blue Shield, UnitedHealthcare, Humana and GEHA. Call us and we will help you make the most of your benefits.

Can you see me today for a toothache?

We offer same-day appointments for toothaches and dental emergencies. Call (435) 628-5505 as early as you can.

What does the $99 new patient special include?

A comprehensive exam, a cleaning and digital X-rays for $99 (a $399 value), plus free teeth whitening. Ask us about any limits when you book.

Do you see kids?

Yes. We are a family office with kids activities and TVs in every treatment room, so visits are easy for the whole family.

Where are you located?

In the Dinosaur Crossing area at 2134 E Riverside Dr, Ste B3, St. George, UT 84790, with easy parking.
